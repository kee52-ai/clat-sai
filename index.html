    Discipline code

    Motivation fades. Discipline carries you.

    1. Show up

    Whatever your mood, open the plan. Even small steps count.

    2. Phone away

    Keep your phone away during study blocks. Focus is a gift to your future.

    3. Fix, don’t cry

    A wrong answer is a lesson. Log it, learn it, never repeat it.

    4. Sleep is study

    Sleep by 10 pm. A tired brain loses marks.

    5. Compete with yesterday

    Not against anyone else. Yesterday’s you versus today’s you.

    6. Trust the process

    You will not jump from 84 to 110 in a day. A little each day, without fail.
